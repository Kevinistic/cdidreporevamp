# CDID car update history

Source: Discord `#update-changelogs` (CDID Official Server), read in full from the first message (1 Feb 2022) to 6 Nov 2026.
Times are as displayed by Discord in the browser used for scraping. The 21 Nov 2025 post says "GMT+7", so assume **WIB (UTC+7)**.

## What counts

- **Included:** updates that add new cars (version drops, weekly drops), plus **map/event launches and changes** (marked `map/event` in Notes). Only the map/event posts I was told to add are listed (Danur Last Chapter, Convoy, Independence Day, Bandalam), plus the ones already in the file; other map/event posts seen in the channel are not added.
- **Excluded:** gacha rerolls, bug fixes, and **rework-only** updates (no new cars). Jobs, build tools and UI feature updates are also excluded unless listed.
- **Rule from the owner:** a "server restart to prepare the weekly drop" notice guarantees an update in that period. After that notice, the Friday drop is at about 19:00.
- **Decision (confirmed):** weekly-drop restart notices with no drop post **count as updates**. See Part 3 for the counted dates.
- **Final:** all open questions are resolved. Rows marked "Excluded" in the Notes column are not updates; every other row in Parts 1 to 3 is.
